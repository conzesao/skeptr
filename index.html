One real run · same question to three providers

"What is the OEM part number for the water inlet valve on a Whirlpool WRF535SMHZ refrigerator?"

Model ARefuted

"The primary OEM part number is W11165546." — plus superseded numbers, revision suffixes, and where to find the rating plate.

W11165546 is a washing-machine inlet valve. The most detailed answer was the wrong one.

Model BUnverified

"The part number is typically W11168740. It's always good to verify with the manufacturer."

That valve is real — but no source confirms it fits this model. Plausible, unproven.

Model CDeclined

"I don't have the part number for that model. Check the parts diagram, or read the number off the valve itself."

Refused to guess — and on this question that was the right call.

What the sources actually say: no manufacturer source lists a valve for the WRF535SMHZ. The part Google puts at the top, WPW10420083, belongs to the WRF535SWHZ — one letter apart, different appliance. Order it and you wait a week for a part that doesn't fit.
